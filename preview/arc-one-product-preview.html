What the click means

Early click

You’re letting go near the top.

Later click

You’re holding on longer into the downswing.

If it clicks near the top, you’re letting go too soon.

The problem it solves

Early release is hard to feel. It’s easy to hear.

If you’ve ever left the range thinking you swung hard and still hit it soft, this is usually why. ARC ONE clicks when you release so you can tell if it happened early or late — then adjust on the next swing.

Usual problem

It feels fast, then it’s gone

A lot of golfers unload early and never notice. The swing feels strong; the ball doesn’t.

With ARC ONE

A simple 5-minute drill

1 min

See where you are now

Take a few easy swings and just listen. Don’t try to fix anything yet.

3 min

Work the click later

Slow the transition a bit. Keep going until the click sits closer to the bottom of the swing.

1 min

Take it to a club

Grab a normal club and make a few swings with that same feeling.

Go slow. You’re training timing, not speed.

Quick read

Push the click down the swing.

Think of the downswing as a short line from the top to the ball. You want the sound near the bottom of that line, not the top.
